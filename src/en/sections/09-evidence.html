09 / READ THE NUMBERS

When someone says it is faster,
ask what they measured

Recent publication and strong evidence are different things. We have not combined different tasks and measurement methods into a single ranking.

2025-07-10 · Randomized experiment
+19%

Conditions where tasks took longer

METR's experiment with early-2025 tools compared 246 tasks completed by 16 experienced open-source developers in their own repositories.

This does not establish the effects of all late-2026 models. The result applies to particular people, tools and tasks.

[[metr2025|Original report]]
2026-02-24 · Follow-up study design report

Measuring newer tools
has become harder too

Discusses selection bias when developers and tasks drop out because people do not want to work without AI, alongside the difficulty of measuring time spent on parallel work.

Do not treat the follow-up's raw numbers as a confirmed speedup. The authors also describe the evidence for estimating the effect size as weak.

[[metr2026|Original report]]
2026-05-11 · Self-report survey
1.4–2×

Respondents' estimates of work value

A survey of 349 technical workers conducted in February–April 2026. The range covers medians from several measures of change in work value. A separate self-reported measure of speed had a median of 3×.

Convenience sampling, selection bias and self-assessment limit the findings. Do not read these as causal multipliers of actual productivity.

A slow baseline can be misleading

Self-reported figures from Mitchell's renderer experiment. The post describes them as results from the same benchmark.

88 msBasic initial implementation
1.5 msAfter agent improvements
0.020 msManually written implementation

Column widths are not proportional to the values. We did not reproduce these measurements and do not generalize them into an AI-versus-human performance comparison.

[[2060088112257372610|May 2026 experiment]]

PR counts and code volume measure output

poteto mentions about 1,150 PRs over roughly 4 days of game development, and a 30× increase in code output in another post. Peter also reports roughly 600 PRs for a migration.

What these numbers alone cannot tell us: how much faster the same scope was completed; the cost of rework, regressions and review; or the increase in user value.

Track four outcomes separately in your own work

Time to completion

Include waiting, review, fixes and rollout—not just implementation.

Correctness and rework

Record acceptance criteria, reproduced errors and reopened issues.

Total cost

Separate token and runtime costs from human management time.

Human understanding

Check whether you can explain causes, contracts and failure conditions.

Learning is a separate outcome. Anthropic's randomized experiment published on 2026-01-29 studied developers learning a new library. The AI group's average immediate quiz score was 50%, compared with 67% for the manual-work group. The task-time difference was not statistically significant. The separate analysis by usage pattern was observational. This short learning experiment cannot be generalized to long-term professional work or all current autonomous agents.
