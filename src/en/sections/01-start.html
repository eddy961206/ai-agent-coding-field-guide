Four ideas to take with you

These are editorial recommendations drawn from comparing several sources. They do not require a particular model or plugin.

Intent

and the acceptance criteria

Add reproduction steps, expected results, and boundaries to “fix this.” Specify the working method only as far as needed.

Before adding more instructions, see whether types, API boundaries, quick checks, or execution tools can prevent the mistake.

Evidence

Support completion reports
with execution evidence

Distinguish changed code, the version actually running, and observed behavior. A single screenshot does not justify broader claims.

the important assumptions

Developer context and judgment are needed to decide whether this is the right product and where performance, compatibility, and permissions matter.
